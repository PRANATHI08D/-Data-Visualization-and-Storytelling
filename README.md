# -Data-Visualization-and-Storytelling
This repository contains a visualization and analysis of a superstore dataset. The visualization aims to provide insights into various aspects of the superstore's sales, profit, and discounts across different product categories and cities.

The visualization provides the following key insights:

* **Sales by Category:** Technology products have the highest total sales, followed by Furniture and then Office Supplies.
* **Profit by Category:** Technology also generates the highest profit, significantly more than Office Supplies and Furniture. Furniture shows the lowest profit among the three categories.
* **Discount by Category:** Office Supplies have the highest total discount applied, followed by Furniture and then Technology. In terms of the percentage of discount relative to sales (indicated in the pie chart labels), Office Supplies also have the highest percentage of discount.
* **Sales and Order Date Trend:** This chart shows the trend of total sales and the count of orders over time. There appear to be peaks and troughs in sales and order volume at different points.
* **Discount by City:** Philadelphia has the highest total discount applied, followed by Houston and Chicago.
